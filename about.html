Our Story

0xdecodeyourself was born from a simple observation: most college students and recent grads feel lost when it comes to their careers. They're overwhelmed by job boards that prioritize salary over values, advice that doesn't fit them, and a lack of mentorship when they need it most.

Sebastian's two decades of experience in innovation and mentorship paired with Angel's fresh perspective and tech-forward approach creates something unique — a platform that combines strategic guidance with technology, human connection with AI innovation, and career exploration with values clarity.

Together, they're building a career coaching platform that treats recent grads as the thoughtful, values-driven people they are. Not just job seekers. Not just resumes. People who deserve to find work that matters.

Our
Mission

Help college students and recent grads decode themselves — and find careers that actually align with who they are, what they value, and what they want to build next.
